# Multi-Store Pharmacy Management System

> **Comprehensive Solution for Pharmacy Chain Operations**
> 
> Advanced platform supporting pharmacist ownership of multiple store locations with mandatory resident pharmacist assignments and centralized management capabilities.

## 👨‍⚕️ **Resident Pharmacist System**

### **Mandatory Assignment Requirement**
Every pharmacy store location must have a designated resident pharmacist responsible for day-to-day pharmaceutical operations and professional oversight.

#### **Resident Pharmacist Qualifications**
```yaml
Professional Requirements:
  PCN License: Valid pharmacist license in good standing
  Experience: Minimum 2 years post-graduation practice
  Competency Assessment: Demonstrated clinical and operational skills
  Professional Development: Commitment to continuing education
  
Personal Requirements:
  Physical Presence: Regular on-site availability during operating hours
  Geographic Proximity: Residence within reasonable distance of store
  Communication Skills: Ability to interact with patients and staff
  Leadership Capability: Team management and decision-making skills
```

#### **Responsibilities & Duties**
```yaml
Clinical Responsibilities:
  Patient Counseling: Medication education and health guidance
  Prescription Verification: Accuracy and appropriateness checking
  Drug Interaction Screening: Safety monitoring and alerts
  Adverse Event Reporting: Pharmacovigilance and safety reporting
  
Operational Responsibilities:
  Staff Supervision: Training and performance management
  Inventory Management: Stock control and quality assurance
  Regulatory Compliance: Adherence to all professional standards
  Quality Control: Maintaining pharmaceutical care standards
  
Administrative Responsibilities:
  Record Keeping: Accurate documentation and reporting
  Financial Oversight: Budget management and cost control
  Customer Relations: Complaint resolution and service excellence
  Professional Development: Continuous learning and improvement
```

### **Assignment Process**
```yaml
Recruitment & Selection:
  1. Job Posting: Internal and external recruitment
  2. Application Review: Qualification assessment
  3. Interview Process: Competency and cultural fit evaluation
  4. Reference Verification: Professional and character references
```

## 🏢 **Store Registration & Setup Process**

### **New Store Registration Workflow**
```yaml
Phase 1: Pre-Registration Requirements
  1. Business Plan Development
     - Market analysis and feasibility study
     - Financial projections and funding plan
     - Location analysis and demographic assessment
     - Competition analysis and differentiation strategy
     
  2. Regulatory Preparation
     - PCN pharmacy license application
     - Business registration and permits
     - Location permits and zoning compliance
     - Insurance coverage and bonding
     
  3. Infrastructure Setup
     - Premises lease/purchase agreement
     - Renovation and equipment installation
     - Security systems and surveillance
     - Technology infrastructure deployment

Phase 2: System Registration
  1. Portal Account Setup
     - Primary pharmacist verification
     - Business documentation upload
     - Location GPS coordinates confirmation
     - Operating hours and service definitions
     
  2. Staff Assignment
     - Resident pharmacist selection and assignment
     - Support staff role definitions
     - Access permission configuration
     - Training schedule development
     
  3. Integration Setup
     - GoMed seller account creation
     - WhatsApp business profile setup
     - Payment gateway configuration
     - Inventory system initialization

Phase 3: Verification & Activation
  1. Location Verification
     - GPS coordinate validation
     - Physical premises inspection
     - Compliance assessment
     - Quality assurance review
     
  2. System Testing
     - Platform functionality verification
     - Integration testing (GoMed, WhatsApp)
     - Staff access confirmation
     - Security protocol validation
     
  3. Official Launch
     - Store activation in system
     - Public announcement and marketing
     - Customer onboarding
     - Continuous monitoring initiation
```

### **Location Requirements & Verification**
```yaml
Physical Requirements:
  Minimum Space: 200 square meters total area
  Dispensing Area: Dedicated and secure pharmaceutical space
  Storage Facilities: Climate-controlled storage areas
  Customer Area: Comfortable waiting and consultation space
  Security Features: Surveillance, alarms, and access control
  
Geographic Requirements:
  Accessibility: Public transportation and vehicle access
  Visibility: Street-level location with clear signage
  Demographics: Adequate population density and healthcare needs
  Competition: Appropriate market spacing from competitors
  Zoning: Commercial or mixed-use zoning compliance
  
Technology Requirements:
  Internet Connectivity: High-speed broadband connection
  Power Supply: Reliable electricity with backup systems
  POS Systems: Modern point-of-sale and inventory management
  Security Systems: CCTV, alarms, and access controls
  Communication: Telephone and mobile network coverage
```

### **Multi-Level Security System**
```yaml
Access Control Matrix:
  Primary Pharmacist: Full access to all stores and systems
  Resident Pharmacist: Complete access to assigned store
  Senior Staff: Limited access to specific store functions
  Support Staff: Role-based access to necessary features
  Temporary Staff: Restricted access with time limitations
  
Security Features:
  Biometric Authentication: Fingerprint or facial recognition
```
